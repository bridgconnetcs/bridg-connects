Cargo valueValor de la carga

Tell us what it's worth.

Dinos cuánto vale.

Covered on value, even on small orders.

Cubierto por valor, aunque el pedido sea chico.

A mixed hardware order is covered the same way as a pallet: against the value you declare at booking. Small does not mean uncovered, and fragile items in the order are not excluded.

Un pedido variado de ferretería se cubre igual que un pallet: contra el valor que declaras al reservar. Chico no significa sin cobertura, y lo frágil del pedido no está excluido.

Coverage is arranged per load against the value you declare, before the driver is dispatched. Declare the value when you book — we cannot cover what we were not told about.

La cobertura se gestiona por carga contra el valor que declaras, antes de despachar al conductor. Declara el valor al reservar — no podemos cubrir lo que no se nos dijo.
