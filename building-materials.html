Building materials are usually low value per pound and high value per pallet, which is exactly why declaring the value matters. Tell us what the load is worth at booking and coverage is arranged against that figure before the driver is dispatched.

Los materiales de construcción suelen valer poco por libra y mucho por pallet, y por eso importa declarar el valor. Dinos cuánto vale la carga al reservar y la cobertura se gestiona contra esa cifra antes de despachar al conductor.

Coverage is arranged per load against the value you declare, before the driver is dispatched. Declare the value when you book — we cannot cover what we were not told about.
